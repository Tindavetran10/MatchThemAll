# Asset licensing: ship only owned, CC0 or OFL/MIT content

**Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** accepted

## Context
The template referenced art, audio, models, fonts and VFX from other publishers' Asset Store packs
(GUIPackCartoon, UI Kawaii, Design Toolbox, BTM, Cosmic, Sci-Fi RTS, 4Tudio, JMO) plus course-provided
SFX. The Asset Store does not allow redistributing other publishers' content, free or paid.

## Decision
- Everything under `Assets/MatchThemAllTemplate` must be made by us, CC0, or permissively licensed
  (MIT/OFL) with its notice in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt`.
- Replacements are generated by dev-only tools in `Assets/_AssetGen/Editor` (menu **Tools ▸ AssetGen**):
  `SfxSynth`, `SpriteGen`, `ModelGen`, `VfxGen`. `AssetGuidSwap` repoints references via `swap-map.txt`.
  `_AssetGen` is **not** exported with the package.
- New assets keep the old geometry/bounds/9-slice borders, so no prefab or scene layout changed.
- PrimeTween and ZLinq stay external dependencies (listed in README), not bundled.

## Consequences
- Before every export: rerun the GUID scan — the only allowed references outside the template are
  TextMesh Pro essentials (`Assets/TextMesh Pro`, shipped by Unity's TMP package).
- To restyle art or sounds, edit the generator and rerun its menu item; GUIDs stay stable.
